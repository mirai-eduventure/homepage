About Mirai

Connecting Japan, Indonesia, and Vietnam through education.

We believe in the power of studying abroad — because it shaped who we are. Now we're building that same chance for the next generation.

Our origin

Three journeys, one shared realization.

Each of our three founders spent time studying abroad. Along the way, we met people from different backgrounds, learned about cultures and beliefs unlike our own, and learned things that reshaped how we saw the world.

We came back with a wider view of what was possible — new choices for the paths our lives could take. It's because of that experience that each of us became who we are today.

"Life-Changing Program" isn't a marketing line for us. It's the exact wish we've been chasing since we started — that every student we work with gets to feel some version of what that experience gave us.

Mirai Eduventure Indonesia was founded in Indonesia in 2023, built on the belief that this kind of opportunity should reach young people as early as possible. The name itself reflects that mission — Mirai (future) combined with Education × Adventure, for those seeking a future built through the adventure of learning.
